Analysis Team

Project Team

This benchmark analysis is supported by a multidisciplinary team spanning aviation perspective, finance and risk input, systems structuring, and research coordination.

The team structure is designed to keep the work practical and decision-oriented. It combines sector context, financial interpretation, technical organization, and research support so the benchmark can be read both strategically and operationally.

The aim is not to present personal resumes, but to show the mix of perspectives contributing to the analysis and why that mix matters for an airline benchmarking exercise.
