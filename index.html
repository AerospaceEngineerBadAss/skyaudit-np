Delay Analysis

Why Flights Are Delayed

Breakdown of delay causes as a share of total delayed departures. Red = Nepal Airlines · Teal = Druk Air.

The standout: Immigration is Nepal's single biggest delay cause at 33% — bigger than engineering, ATC, weather and operations combined. Druk Air has zero immigration delays because Paro Airport handles far lower passenger volumes. This is a CAAN / Ministry infrastructure problem, not an airline problem.
